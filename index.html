About

I am a theoretical physicist working on quantum gravity and black hole physics. Since October 2024 I am a postdoc in the Quantum Gravity group at the Niels Bohr Institute in Copenhagen.

I obtained my PhD summa cum laude at SISSA, Trieste (2024), with a thesis on “Beyond Lorentz invariance: a journey from Analogue to Hořava Gravity” under the supervision of Stefano Liberati. Before that, I studied physics at the University of Pisa, where I received both my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees with 110/110 cum laude.

My work combines two perspectives: a top-down one, asking how the ultraviolet structure of gravity shapes low-energy physics, and a bottom-up one, asking which features of black hole physics can be understood without knowing the microscopic theory.
